General warnings
----------------

.. Note:: Any use of the |rc_viscore_camera| in noncompliance with these warnings is inappropriate and
   may cause injury or damage as well as void the warranty.

.. Warning::
   * The |rc_viscore_camera| is protected according to IP54. High humidity or temperature can damage the device. Do not
     operate in an environment where combustible or explosive fumes may occur.
   * The |rc_viscore_camera| needs to be properly mounted before use.
   * All cable sets need to be secured to the |rc_viscore_camera| and the mount.
   * Cables must not be longer than 30 m. The 8-Pin power supply cable must not be longer than 15 m.
   * Power to the |rc_viscore_camera| must be supplied through an appropriate, separate DC power source.
   * An appropriate power supply, which conforms to the EN 62368-1 standard must be used to supply power to the |rc_viscore_camera|.
   * Check polarity and connections.
   * The |rc_viscore_camera|'s housing must be grounded.

.. Warning::
   * The |rc_viscore_camera|'s and any related equipment's safety guidelines must always be satisfied.
   * The |rc_viscore_camera| does not fall under the purview of the machinery or medical directives.

.. rubric::
  Risk assessment and final application:
  The |rc_viscore_camera| may be used :mvexclude:`together with an |rc_cube|` with a robot. Robot,
  |rc_viscore_camera|, :mvexclude:`|rc_cube|,` and any other equipment used in the final
  application must be evaluated with a risk assessment. The system integrator's duty is to
  ensure respect for all local safety measures and regulations. Depending on the application,
  there may be risks that need additional protection/safety measures.

Intended use
------------

The |rc_viscore_camera| is an industrial 3D vision camera that is intended for integration into automation 
and robotics applications. Their purpose is the acquisition of image data and it is intended to be connected 
to a :mvexclude:`|company| |rc_cube| or` host computer.

This product is designed and intended to be operated in a trusted environment with a direct, 
point-to-point (1:1) connection to the :mvexclude:`|company| |rc_cube| or` host computer. In this configuration, 
the devices are not intended to be connected to, or made accessible from, an open or untrusted network.

The provided GigE Vision interfaces is an established industry standard that does not support encryption or 
authentication. Making this interface available outside a trusted, isolated network may expose the system to 
cybersecurity risks and is outside the intended use of the product.

The operator is responsible for ensuring that the operating environment matches these conditions, including 
physical access control to the device, network isolation, and secure configuration of the host system where 
applicable. Operating the product outside its intended use, in particular by exposing unsecured interfaces 
to an untrusted network, may compromise the security and reliability of the system and is not covered by 
the product's cybersecurity risk assessment.

.. Warning:: The |rc_viscore_camera| is **NOT** intended for safety critical applications.

.. Warning:: The |rc_viscore_camera| with standard optics is **NOT** to be used in dynamic environments or mounted to a robot end-effector.

The |rc_viscore_camera| may be used only within the scope of its technical specification. Any other use of the
product is deemed unintended use. |company| will not be liable for any damages resulting from any
improper or unintended use.

.. Warning:: Always comply with local and/or national laws, regulations and directives on automation
   safety and general machine safety.
